A FEW THINGS, ANSWERED.

Big picture.
Clear details.

What can I advertise on?+

Explore billboard and digital-screen inventory, metro and train media, and connected social and search workflows. Television and OTT are part of our roadmap. Contact us to check availability for your campaign.

Can one campaign use several channels?+

That’s the idea: one campaign plan with creative adapted to each channel. Each provider still has its own formats, permissions, review process and billing requirements.

I own advertising space. How do I join?+

Email info@primeboards.in with your business name, cities and media types. We’ll discuss onboarding, business verification and listing your inventory.

Are all integrations live?+

No. Availability varies by channel and account access. TV and OTT connections are planned. We’ll confirm supported integrations and inventory before you commit to a campaign.
